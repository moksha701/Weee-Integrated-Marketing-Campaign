# Taste of Home, Delivered. — Weee! Integrated Marketing Campaign

## 📌 Project Overview

A conceptual integrated marketing campaign developed for **Weee!**, America's largest online Asian supermarket, focused on increasing awareness and purchase intent among Indian-Americans.

The campaign is built around a simple insight: **people don't miss ingredients—they miss the feeling of home.** Through emotionally driven storytelling, lifecycle email marketing, and data-informed campaign planning, this concept positions Weee! as the easiest way to recreate authentic Indian meals using trusted brands delivered directly to customers' doors.

This is a self-initiated conceptual project created to demonstrate **brand strategy, integrated marketing, CRM, customer journey mapping, and marketing analytics.**

---

## 💡 The Big Idea

**"Ghar Ka Swad. Delivered."**

For many Indian-Americans, a familiar dish isn't just food—it's a memory.

A single bite of homemade food can transport someone back to their childhood kitchen, a family dinner, or a recipe learned from their parents. This campaign transforms those emotional moments into a modern shopping experience by connecting authentic Indian brands with the convenience of home delivery.

Instead of selling groceries, the campaign sells the feeling of home.
